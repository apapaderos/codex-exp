# Archive entries

The archive is how Loom avoids starting cold. Secondary research greps these frontmatters
before anything else, so retrieval quality depends on consistent values.

## Frontmatter vocabulary

- `client`: the legal or commonly used client name, consistently spelled.
- `sector`: one of banking, insurance, retail, energy, shipping, telecom, public, health,
  real-estate, professional-services, industrial, other.
- `challenge_type`: one of operating-model, customer-experience, employee-experience,
  digital-product, strategy, ai-adoption, process, data, culture, other.
- `tags`: 3 to 8 lower-case keywords (e.g. onboarding, handover, stores).

## Landed

Something landed when tracking shows it in use or its KPI met, with evidence. A published
document that nobody uses did not land. List what did not land too, with why if known.

## Lessons worth keeping

Keep lessons a future team can act on:
- how this organisation decides, and who must be in the room;
- constraints that were not in the brief but shaped everything;
- what was tried and failed, and why;
- which triage calls were wrong in hindsight.
Write about roles and patterns, not judgements of individuals.

## Backfill

For past engagements, write the same format from whatever records exist, and mark
uncertain fields in the body.
