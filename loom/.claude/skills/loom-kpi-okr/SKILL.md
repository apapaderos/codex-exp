# KPIs and OKRs

## KPIs

- Measure the outcome the problem named, not the activity ("days from start date to working
  unsupervised", not "template published").
- Each KPI: `metric` (unit included), `baseline` (value and where it came from), `target`,
  `by` (date).
- Baseline from evidence: research responses, documents, tracking updates. If none, write
  `unknown - measure by <date>` and name the data source.
- One to three KPIs per item. At least one must be observable within 90 days.
- For a decision item, the KPI is usually "decision recorded by <date>" plus the first
  outcome it should move.

## OKR

One objective (qualitative, in the owner's language) and two or three key results, which are
the KPIs with targets.

## Status

- not_started: no evidence of activity.
- in_progress: activity evidenced, targets not yet reached.
- adopted: the change is in use, targets not yet measured.
- at_risk: due date close with no progress, or an update reports a blocker.
- stalled: no update for 30 days past due.
- met: every KPI reached its target, with the update quoted.

## Updates

On each update, keep a dated history in the body: `- 2026-11-20: current 3/5 teams (source: update-11-20.txt)`.
