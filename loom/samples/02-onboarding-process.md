# Store onboarding process (current, v3)

Owner: HR Operations. Last reviewed March 2025.

1. HR issues the contract and the welcome pack.
2. IT is notified by email to prepare a laptop and accounts.
3. The store manager assigns shifts for week one.
4. Till certification happens in week three, run by the regional trainer.

Known issue: step 2 has no service level; step 3 does not reference the welcome pack.
